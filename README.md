## What the doctrine covers

- Scope and preconditions (owned accounts, human review, stop on platform defenses)
- Configure for your brand
- Browser architecture
- Deliberate browser behavior
- Cron-by-cron guide
- Recovery and failure handling
- Anti-patterns
- Final doctrine
- First-run checklist
- Reply skeletons (drop-in templates)
- FAQ

The full, load-bearing detail lives in [`SKILL.md`](./SKILL.md).
